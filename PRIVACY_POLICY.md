## Android Permissions

The app requires the following Android permissions for drone control functionality:

### **INTERNET**
- **Purpose**: To communicate with your drone via WiFi network
- **Usage**: Sends flight commands and receives telemetry data
- **Data**: Only transmitted to your local drone (LiteWing ESP32-S3)
- **Storage**: No internet data is stored

### **ACCESS_WIFI_STATE**
- **Purpose**: To detect available WiFi networks and find your drone
- **Usage**: Scans for networks containing "LiteWing", "FLYQ", or "ESP32"
- **Data**: Network names only, no credentials
- **Storage**: Not stored

### **CHANGE_WIFI_STATE**
- **Purpose**: To connect to your drone's WiFi network
- **Usage**: Initiates connection to drone network
- **Data**: No data transmitted
- **Storage**: Not stored

### **ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE**
- **Purpose**: To monitor connection status with your drone
- **Usage**: Displays connection quality and status
- **Data**: Local network status only
- **Storage**: Not stored

### **VIBRATE**
- **Purpose**: To provide haptic feedback when you press buttons
- **Usage**: Vibrates device for better user experience
- **Data**: None
- **Storage**: Not stored

**Important**: All permissions are used solely for drone control functionality. No data leaves your device except for local WiFi communication with your drone.
